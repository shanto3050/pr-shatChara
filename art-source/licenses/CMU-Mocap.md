# CMU motion data

Official source: [CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/). Terms checked 29 October 2026 at the [official FAQ](http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php).

The FAQ grants: “The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed without permission.”

Included numerical source: subject 02 skeleton, take 02_01 (walk), take 02_03 (run/jog), recorded at 120 Hz. Exact download URLs and SHA-256 digests are in `art-source/motion/cmu/manifest.json`. These are recorded adult motions, retargeted for a provisional game character; they are not a child-specific capture or game-specific throw/stack recording.

Credit: motion recordings from CMU Graphics Lab; database funding included NSF EIA-0196217. The ASF/AMC converter, loop selection, heading normalization, runtime retargeting and contact solver are project code. The data is not labeled CC0; its reuse grant is the FAQ above.
