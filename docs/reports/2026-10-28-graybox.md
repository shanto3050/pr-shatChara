28 October 2026, client/desktop date. Phone logcat timestamps differ from the client clock; do not interpret them as production dates.

## Delivered scope

Source commit **12eeada** adds an actual 4 vs 4 scene with touch sticks, opening throws, a controlled ballistic ball, swept contacts, pickup/carry/ordered placement, AI carriers and defenders, outs, player transfer, turns, scoring, pause and rematch. The foundation diagnostic scene remains available. Read [controls and implementation limits](../playable-graybox.md).

This is simple graybox geometry, not the realistic-art milestone. No village art, finished characters, animation, audio or final balance is claimed.

## Device issue found and fixed

The editor test passed while the first phone candidate printed “Can't add component because class 'CapsuleCollider' doesn't exist” and similar primitive errors. Added link.xml roots for the collider and mesh component types requested by runtime primitives, then rebuilt and retested on the phone. [Unity documents runtime primitive component requirements](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/GameObject.CreatePrimitive.html) and [linker preservation](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/Manual/managed-code-stripping-preserving.html).

Also fixed player-array collision priority by resolving the earliest swept intersection. Teammates now prioritize the next required stone more strongly; the substantial change in scenario outcome reinforces the need for actual balance work.
